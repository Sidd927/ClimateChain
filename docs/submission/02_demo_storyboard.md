# 02 — Demo Storyboard

**One sentence:** a real retirement, two synthetic companies, one overlap the Ruler shows and the contract refuses.
**Arc:** hook → insight → product → proof → enforcement → limits → closing line. Seven scenes, 3:27 in the reference take.
**Visual rule:** every proof point is shown live in the running app, not on a slide. The film has no slides.

## Shot grammar
- One continuous screen recording; cuts happen only at route changes.
- The pointer moves deliberately and always lands on the thing the voice names.
- Scroll with smooth scrolling; no jumpy wheel scrolling.
- Never show terminals, `.env` files, wallet keys, or browser chrome beyond the tab.
- The overlap gets the longest hold of the film (4d): name it, point at both ends, then click.

## What is deliberately left out
Challenge/resolve/withdraw flows, gas charts, the registry audit (11,631 cross-batch artefacts) and AI extraction (not configured). Each is in the repo and in `04_judge_q_and_a.md`; none is needed to make the central point in under four minutes.
